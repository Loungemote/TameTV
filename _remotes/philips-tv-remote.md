## What you can do

Free, with no ads:

- D-pad, OK, Back and Home, volume, mute, channel, power and playback keys
- Turn the TV on over Wi-Fi, on TVs that support it
- A simple layout with big keys
- Every TV at home, a tap apart
- After a TV cleanup: a check that casting and the remote still work after an ADB tool
- Demo TV, to try the remote without a TV

With TameTV Pro:

- Swipe touchpad for one-handed navigation
- Casting: photos, slideshows, videos, files and links
- Quiet Ads: turns the TV down when a loud ad starts, listening for loudness only
- On iPhone and iPad: a Lock Screen remote, Help Family over FaceTime, and Siri and Shortcuts to turn the TV on or off or mute it by voice

TameTV Pro is a weekly plan with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, a yearly plan or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices appear before you pay. Some features depend on the TV model and its software.

## Set up in three steps

1. Connect your phone and the TV to the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks often block the connection.
2. Open TameTV. It finds the TV on your network by itself.
3. Titan OS TVs show a code on the screen. Type it in the app. Saphi TVs need no pairing.

Pairing happens only the first time. Next time, the app reconnects when you open it. If the TV does not appear, choose **Add by IP address** and type the address from the TV's network settings.

## Turn the TV on from your phone

Many Philips TVs can start from the network. Look for a network standby or Wake-on-LAN setting in the TV's settings.

## Good to know

- On Titan OS and Saphi, TameTV sends remote keys. The keyboard and the app launcher are not available.
- Philips TVs with Google TV or Android TV connect as [Google TV](../android-tv-remote/).
- Your phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. TameTV does not use infrared, and it cannot control the TV from outside your home.
